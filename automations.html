Questions

Good to know.

Do I need any technical knowledge?

No. We set everything up and look after it. You just keep using your email, WhatsApp and phone like normal.

Will it work with the apps I already use?

Almost always. We connect to common email, calendar, spreadsheet, messaging, shop and payment tools. If something can't connect, we'll tell you before you pay.

Is my customer data safe?

Your automations run on your own accounts, and you stay the owner of all your data. We only get the access needed to set things up, and you can remove it any time.

What does the monthly fee cover?

Running costs on our side, monitoring, fixing anything when an app changes, and small updates like new prices, hours or wording.

Can you do it faster?

Yes. Standard delivery is 5–7 days. Express delivery gets your automation live in 3–5 days for an extra $400.

Can I cancel?

Yes, monthly plans can be cancelled with 30 days' notice. Anything built on your accounts stays yours.

WhatsApp and Instagram — anything special?

Automated messaging needs a verified business account. We guide you through verification, which usually takes a few days, and only message people who contacted you or opted in.
