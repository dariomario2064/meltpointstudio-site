      Insights · Pricing
      How much does a 3D website cost?
      
      
      A 3D website puts a real-time object or scene at the heart of your page — something visitors can scroll through, spin and explore. Because it's built and lit like a product shoot, it takes more craft than a regular site, and the price reflects that.
      
      
      What drives the price
      The complexity of the 3D scene, how much it moves with scroll, interactions like hold-to-reveal, sound, and how many pages surround it. A single striking centerpiece costs far less than a full 3D world.
      
      
      What you can expect
      Freelance 3D sites commonly start in the low thousands, while agencies and award-level studios charge much more. At Meltpoint Studio, 3D websites start at $3,500, and you can add a 3D centerpiece to an existing site from $1,200.
      
      
      How to budget
      Decide what the 3D should achieve — wow first impressions, explain a product, or show off your brand — then pick the smallest scene that does that job beautifully.

      Insights · Planning
      Landing page or full website?
      
      
      A landing page is one focused page with one goal: sign up, buy, book or enquire. A full website has several pages that explain your business in depth.
      
      
      Choose a landing page if…
      You're launching one product, running ads or a campaign, collecting a waitlist, or need something live fast on a smaller budget.
      
      
      Choose a full website if…
      You offer several services, want to rank on Google for different topics, or customers need about, gallery, pricing and contact pages before they trust you.
      
      
      The smart path
      Many businesses start with a landing page and grow it into a full site later — nothing is wasted.
